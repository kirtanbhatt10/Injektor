# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Injektor to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, experience level or identity. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for all.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before drawing conclusions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Sharing exploits, credentials or prompts aimed at systems the author is not authorized to test.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@kirtanbhatt10](https://github.com/kirtanbhatt10). Reports are handled promptly and as confidentially as possible. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful to the community.
